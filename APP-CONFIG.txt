HBOMB R.G. V3.2 DEPLOY APP LINKS

Edit HBOMB_APP_CONFIG near the bottom of index.html.
For each app, fill in launchUrl and/or downloadUrl.
Leave a URL empty to keep that button safely disabled.

Example:
launchUrl: "https://apps.hbombrg.com/blue-wave/"
downloadUrl: "https://downloads.hbombrg.com/blue-wave/setup.exe"

No interface-engine edits are required to change these links.
